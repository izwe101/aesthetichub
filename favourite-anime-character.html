Favourite anime characters

Sailor Moon

Usagi Tsukino is a cheerful and clumsy teenage girl who discovers she is Sailor Moon, a magical warrior who fights evil and protects the world. With her iconic white hair buns and eternal sailor suit, she represents love, justice, and friendship.

Series: Sailor Moon | Age: 14 | Role: Magical Warrior

Naruto Uzumaki

A determined and optimistic ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village. Despite being ostracized for housing a powerful demon within him, Naruto never gives up and strives to become Hokage, the strongest ninja of all.

Series: Naruto | Age: 16 | Role: Ninja & Hero
